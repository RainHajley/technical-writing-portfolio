# How to Use Grammarly in Google Docs

This guide shows you how to install the Grammarly browser extension so you can get real-time spelling and grammar suggestions directly while writing in Google Docs.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following:
* A **Google account** (to access Google Docs).
* A **desktop web browser** (such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge).
* A **Grammarly account** (either a free or paid premium tier).

---

## Step-by-Step Installation and Setup

### Step 1: Add Grammarly to Your Browser
First, you need to add the Grammarly extension utility to your preferred web browser.

1. Open your browser's extension marketplace (e.g., the **Chrome Web Store**).
2. Type `Grammarly` into the search field and press **Enter**.
3. Select **Grammarly for Chrome** (or your respective browser name) from the search results.
4. Click the **Add to Chrome** button.
5. Click **Add extension** when the confirmation pop-up window appears.

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Account
Link the browser extension to your personal Grammarly account to sync your custom dictionary and writing preferences.

1. Click the **Extensions** icon (which looks like a puzzle piece) in the top-right corner of your browser toolbar.
2. Select **Grammarly** from the dropdown menu.
3. Click **Log In** on the small pop-up interface.
4. Provide your account credentials on the redirected Grammarly authentication page to complete the login.

### Step 3: Enable Grammarly Inside Google Docs
Once installed and authenticated, you can activate the writing assistant interface inside any document.

1. Open any document interface inside **Google Docs**.
2. Locate the floating green or red **Grammarly status circle** in the bottom-right corner of the page canvas.
3. Click the status circle to expand the comprehensive **Grammarly Sidebar**.
4. Verify that the toggle switch next to **Check for suggestions on this domain** is set to **ON**.

## Troubleshooting Quick Fixes

| Symptom | Root Cause | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The Grammarly circle does not appear in Google Docs. | The extension is globally disabled in your browser settings. | 1. Click the browser puzzle piece icon.<br>2. Locate Grammarly and verify the toggle switch is enabled.<br>3. Refresh the active Google Docs browser page. |
| The real-time suggestions are freezing or flashing on-screen. | A conflicting active extension (such as an aggressive ad-blocker) is interfering. | 1. Disable other browser extensions sequentially to isolate the conflicting utility.<br>2. Alternatively, test performance by launching Google Docs inside an Incognito/Private window. |
