# How to Use Grammarly in Google Docs

## Overview This guide explains how to install and configure the Grammarly browser extension to receive real-time spelling and grammar suggestions directly within Google Docs. 
## Prerequisites Before you begin, ensure you have the following:
* A Google account
* A Grammarly account (Free or Premium)
* A supported desktop web browser (Google Chrome, Firefox, or Microsoft Edge)
---
## Step 1: Add Grammarly to Your Browser 1. Open your browser's extension marketplace (e.g., the **Chrome Web Store**). 
1. Type `Grammarly` into the search bar and press **Enter**.
1. Select **Grammarly for Chrome** from the search results.
1. Click **Add to Chrome**, then click **Add extension** when the confirmation dialog appears.
2. ## Step 2: Sign In to Your Account
1. Click the **Extensions** icon (puzzle piece) in the top-right corner of your browser toolbar.
1. Select **Grammarly**.
1. Click **Log In** in the pop-up window.
1. Enter your credentials on the Grammarly login page.
## Step 3: Enable Grammarly in Google Docs 
1. Open any document in **Google Docs**.
1. Click the green or red floating **Grammarly widget** in the bottom-right corner of the page to open the sidebar.
1. Verify that the toggle switch next to **Check for suggestions on this domain** is turned **ON**.
---
## Troubleshooting | Problem | Cause | Resolution | 
| :--- | :--- | :--- | 
| The Grammarly widget does not appear in Google Docs. | The extension is disabled in your browser settings. | Click the browser **Extensions** icon, verify Grammarly is toggled on, and refresh the Google Docs page. |
| Suggestions freeze or flash continuously. | An active browser extension (such as an ad-blocker) is conflicting with Grammarly. | Disable other extensions one by one to isolate the conflict, or open Google Docs in an **Incognito** window. | 

> [!NOTE] > If troubleshooting steps do not resolve your issue, ensure your browser is updated to the latest stable version.
